Common questions

Quick answers.

Is Nouriva AI a medical app? Should I follow its advice over my doctor?

No. Nouriva AI is a nutrition and lifestyle tool, not a diagnostic or treatment device. All insights are based on peer-reviewed nutritional science and designed to support, not replace, conversations with your healthcare provider. When in doubt, ask your doctor.

How accurate is the food scanning AI?

Our model identifies a wide range of foods — Indian, Asian, Mediterranean, Western — with high accuracy. For mixed dishes (thalis, combos, restaurant meals), it returns a best-estimate breakdown of the components. You can always correct or refine a scan to improve future accuracy.

What happens to my lab reports after I upload them?

We extract the out-of-range markers (HbA1c, cholesterol, vitamins, thyroid, etc.) and store them as structured data. The original PDF or image is encrypted, kept private to your account, and can be deleted anytime. We never share lab data with third parties — full stop.

Do I need to enter my conditions to get useful results?

You'll get useful results either way — but adding your conditions makes the analysis 5–10× more personal. A samosa scores differently for someone with Type 2 diabetes vs. someone managing iron-deficiency anaemia. The more context you give, the sharper the verdict.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your App Store or Google Play subscription settings — takes 30 seconds. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. Your data stays in your account unless you delete it.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No personal data is used to train AI models without explicit, opt-in consent. Your scans, labs, and conditions are private to you. Anonymous, aggregated insights may inform future features, but never tied to your identity.
